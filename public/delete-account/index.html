Delete your account

This page explains how to request deletion of your CareVo Skip account (com.carevo.customer_app), and exactly what is removed and what is kept.

Option 1 — From inside the app

This is the fastest route, and it takes effect immediately.

  • Open CareVo Skip and make sure you are signed in.
  • Tap the person icon in the top-right corner of the screen. This opens the Account screen.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  • Read the summary of what will be erased, then tap Continue.
  • Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

You will be signed out straight away and returned to the sign-in screen. The deletion is applied at that moment — there is no waiting period and no undo.

Option 2 — By email

If you have uninstalled the app, cannot sign in, or would simply rather not use the in-app route, email us and we will process the deletion for you:

Email adithya@carevo.co.in

Send the request from the email address on your account if you signed in with Google. Otherwise, include the phone number you used to sign in, so we can find the right account. We may ask you to confirm ownership before proceeding — this protects you from someone else deleting your account.

We respond to deletion requests within 30 days, as stated in our Privacy Policy.

What actually happens

We want to be straightforward about this, because "delete" can imply more than any system with accounting obligations can honestly do.

Your account is anonymised, not erased row-and-branch. Every piece of information that identifies you is removed or overwritten, and the emptied account record is kept only as an unusable placeholder holding past order records together. Those order records remain, stripped of any link to you.

Permanently removed

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your Google sign-in link, replaced with a value that can never match a real account
  • Your saved sign-in — you cannot log back in afterwards
  • Your notification device token, and your notification history
  • Your loyalty points balance, reset to zero
  • Any unused coupons and any active membership period

Retained, with no link to you

  • Past order records and their line items — what was ordered, when, and for how much
  • Payment and points ledger entries — references and amounts only

These are kept because restaurants need them for their tax, accounting and legal obligations, and because they are the record of settlement if a payment or refund is ever disputed. They carry no name, phone number or email once your account is anonymised, so they can no longer be traced back to you.

Note that we never held your card number, UPI PIN, CVV or bank credentials in the first place — those stay with our payment processor and were never ours to delete. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

After deletion

  • Signing in with the same phone number or Google account is not possible — the old account can never be recovered or reactivated.
  • You are welcome to sign up again at any time. That creates a completely new account, starting from zero points, with no connection to the old one.
